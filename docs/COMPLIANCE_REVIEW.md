# California Compliance Review Register (Phase 0)

**These items are flagged, not decided.** Nothing below is legal advice or a compliance determination. Each item needs review by a qualified California employment/privacy attorney and/or licensed professional before the related feature launches. The platform must not represent itself as determining anyone's legal eligibility.

| # | Area | Issue to review | Product impact / proposed safeguard | Blocks phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BSIS Guard Registration (Bus. & Prof. Code §7580 et seq.) | How to verify guard cards: BSIS public license lookup (breeze.ca.gov / DCA search) vs. document review vs. data feed. Is automated lookup permitted under DCA terms? Re-verification cadence? | `credential_verifications` requires method + source_reference + human verifier; never auto-verify from a user upload. | 2 |
| 2 | Exposed Firearm Permit, Baton permit, BSIS training certs | Armed work requires a valid guard registration plus a firearm permit tied to specific weapons/calibers. Are caliber/weapon restrictions needed in eligibility? | Armed jobs need both verified credentials in the deterministic filter. Caliber details are a possible future field. | 2, 6 |
| 3 | PPO licensing | Who may lawfully hire and deploy guards: licensed PPOs vs. proprietary private security employers (PSE registration) vs. others. Can a non-PPO business post armed/unarmed guard shifts? | Employer verification captures license type/number. Staff verifies before publishing. Gating rules per org type need counsel's input. | 3 |
| 4 | Platform role / "staffing" status | Could the marketplace itself be treated as a PPO, employment agency, or staffing firm (licensing, joint-employer exposure)? | Position as a marketplace or listing service and avoid dispatching or directing work until counsel advises. | 0, 9 |
| 5 | Worker classification (AB5 / Labor Code §2775, Dynamex ABC test) | Guards placed by PPOs are generally employees of the PPO. Any 1099 or contractor framing, "contract opportunities," or platform-paid workers raise ABC-test risk. | No worker payouts (spec). Job posts must name the employing entity. Copy must not imply the platform employs workers. | 3, 9 |
| 6 | Off-duty law enforcement secondary employment | Agency policies and MOUs, Gov. Code §1126 (incompatible activities), POST/department approvals, use of uniform/equipment, conflicts with peace-officer status, PC 830 considerations. | Separate workflow (`le_officer_profiles`, `le_status`). Never imply registration authorizes work. Grok makes no determinations. Surface items for human review. | 11 |
| 7 | Background checks (FCRA, CA ICRAA Civ. Code §1786, CCRAA) | If the platform orders or furnishes background reports, it may become a CRA or user with disclosure, authorization, and adverse-action duties. BSIS already conducts DOJ/FBI checks for guard registration. | Do not run or store background checks in v1. Rely on the BSIS license status. Revisit with counsel. | 2, 12 |
| 8 | Fair Chance Act (Gov. Code §12952) and local ordinances (LA, SF) | Employers with 5+ employees cannot ask about conviction history before a conditional offer. Platform forms and AI must not solicit it. | No criminal-history fields. Filter AI drafts and employer messages for prohibited inquiries. Add guidance to job-post templates. | 3, 7 |
| 9 | FEHA / anti-discrimination; AI hiring rules (CRD regs on automated-decision systems, eff. Oct 2025) | AI matching or ranking could be an "automated-decision system." Anti-bias testing, records retention (4 yrs), notice. | Deterministic, explainable eligibility. No protected characteristics in features. Log all AI rankings. Humans make every decision. Plan a bias audit. | 6 |
| 10 | CCPA/CPRA (incl. employee/applicant data), CPPA ADMT regulations | Notice at collection, sensitive PI (driver's license, government IDs, precise geolocation), right to know/delete/correct, limit use of SPI, data-processing agreements with xAI/Stripe/Resend/Twilio, risk assessments, ADMT notices. | Approximate location only. Minimize ID numbers. Sub-processor DPAs. DSAR workflow. Do not send SPI to the AI provider. | 1, 6 |
| 11 | Document retention & deletion | Retention periods for license copies, LE approvals, messages, audit logs, applications (FEHA 4-year record retention for applicant data), vs. data minimization and deletion requests. | Define a retention schedule. Archive and purge jobs. Legal-hold flag. | 2, 5 |
| 12 | Pay transparency (Labor Code §432.3, SB 1162) | Employers with 15+ employees must include a pay scale in job postings, including via third parties. | Require a pay range or rate on every job (schema has pay_min/max). Validate before publish. | 3 |
| 13 | Wage & hour | Minimum wage (state and local), overtime, meal/rest breaks, split shifts in multi-day/recurring assignments. Pay display must not imply a guaranteed wage set by the platform. | Display only employer-entered pay. Warn when below the applicable minimum (local tables need maintenance). | 3 |
| 14 | SMS/phone marketing (TCPA, CTIA, A2P 10DLC), email (CAN-SPAM) | Express consent for urgent-shift SMS. Opt-out handling. Quiet hours. | Explicit opt-in captured with timestamp. STOP handling. `notification_preferences`. | 8 |
| 15 | Reviews & defamation; Consumer Review Fairness Act | Moderation process, takedown and dispute, Section 230 posture. | Moderated reviews, dispute flow, no publishing of flagged content. | 13 |
| 16 | Payments / subscriptions (CA Auto-Renewal Law, Bus. & Prof. Code §17600, amended 2024) | Clear disclosures, affirmative consent, easy online cancellation, renewal reminders. | Stripe Checkout disclosures plus self-serve cancellation in the Customer Portal. | 9 |
| 17 | Accessibility (ADA / Unruh Civil Rights Act) | Website accessibility litigation risk in CA. | WCAG 2.2 AA target. Audit in Phase 15. | 15 |
| 18 | Insurance | General liability, E&O, cyber. Whether employers must carry required insurance (PPOs must have liability insurance under BSIS rules). | Possibly collect insurance certificates during employer verification. | 3, 9 |
| 19 | Data security breach law (Civ. Code §1798.82, §1798.150) | Licenses and IDs are PI subject to breach notification and the private right of action. | Encryption, private buckets, signed URLs, audit, incident-response plan. | 1, 12 |
